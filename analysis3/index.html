Perspective

We can try to look at all the words, all at once

By representing sentences as small segments, we can visualize the entire diary corpus as a single document landscape.

But this isn’t very meaningful for us

In this zoomed-out state, the text is anonymous. We see the length and volume, but the internal "mode" of the writing is hidden.

The Model

Now, what if we assign each sentence a label?

By categorizing sentences based on writing mode—Social, Event, or Reflection—the text transforms from a grey mass into a structured dataset.

Then, our zoomed out view becomes quite valuable

Visual patterns emerge. We can identify dense clusters of political anxiety or seasons of social reprieve at a single glance.

Notice patterns and click on sentences to return to text

The visual acts as a portal. By clicking a segment, we zoom directly into the archival entry to read the author's original words.
